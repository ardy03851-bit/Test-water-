🌊 Lagoon Lab — how to play

  • Throw: drag anywhere & release to hurl balls. Tap water for a plop. Pick ball types in the left dock.
  • Ripple: poke & drag the water to sculpt real propagating waves.
  • Move: drag islands to rearrange the lagoon. Water, foam & palms follow.
  • Magnet: hold to bend gravity — balls, spray and embers swirl around your finger.
  • Presets: one-tap moods at the bottom — try ⛈ Storm, then 🌙 Night water glow.
  • ⚙️ panel: ~25 sliders for ocean, sky, weather, islands & FX. Everything morphs live.
  • Pinch the water with two fingers in Ripple mode for a double splash. Storms shake coconuts loose! 🥥
